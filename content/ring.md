Most of the personal web is hosted *somewhere else* — a platform builds it, a CDN serves
it, a company owns the plug. Nothing wrong with that, but there's a smaller group of
people who run the actual machine, and who kept going after the web page: their own git,
their own chat, their own mail, their own game server.

This is a ring for them. If you own the metal, come in.
