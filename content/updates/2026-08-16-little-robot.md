A 1.5B model on its own container, answering questions about this site. It runs on a Xeon
from 2013 at about eleven tokens a second, which is slow enough to be honest about what it
is. It reads my pages and nothing else, has no tools, and occasionally walks across its own
title bar to get your attention.
