The [fishkeeping hobby](/about/) escalated until it became a real storefront: livestock and
equipment both, the full range. As of August 2026 it runs **fully self-hosted** in its own
container on [the node](/projects/homelab/) — migrated off Shopify, payments through
Stripe, no platform between the store and the fish. Still a work in progress, and it stays
its own project with its own repo — this page just points at it.
